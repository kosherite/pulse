About
How the scores work

Recovery (0 to 100) compares last night's heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and skin temperature with your previous 30 nights. Strain (0 to 21) weights each minute of the day by how hard your heart worked. Sleep score (0 to 100) combines time asleep against your need, efficiency, deep and REM share, and regular timing. Sleep stages follow the open-source NOOP method. Health watch starts after 14 nights and flags when resting heart rate, HRV and skin temperature move together the way they do before illness. These are estimates, not WHOOP's figures or a diagnosis.

Your data

The strap records once a second and holds about three days. Pulse collects those readings over Bluetooth while it is open in Bluefy and stores them only on this iPhone. Back up now and then: deleting Bluefy or its website data deletes them. Restore accepts a Pulse backup or the history file from the Mac.

Apple Health shortcut

Build it once in the Shortcuts app. Create a shortcut named exactly Pulse to Health and add, in order:

  1. Get Clipboard
  2. Get Dictionary from Input (input: Clipboard)
  3. Get Dictionary Value: Value for key hr in Dictionary
  4. Repeat with Each item in Dictionary Value
  5. Inside the repeat: Get Dictionary Value for key d in Repeat Item, then for key v in Repeat Item
  6. Still inside: Log Health Sample, type Heart Rate, Value = v, Date = d
  7. After End Repeat, repeat steps 3 to 6 with key rhr (Resting Heart Rate) and key hrv (Heart Rate Variability), picking the Dictionary from step 2 again.

Run it once from Shortcuts and allow Health access. After that, Send to Health opens it for you.
