How Pulse works
Scores

Recovery (0 to 100%) compares last night's heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and skin temperature with your previous 30 nights. Strain (0 to 21) weights each minute of the day by how hard your heart worked. Sleep score (0 to 100) combines time asleep against your need, efficiency, deep and REM share, and regular timing. These are independent estimates, not WHOOP's figures.

Sleep

Pulse finds your main sleep from stillness and heart rate, then labels each minute awake, light, deep or REM, following the open-source NOOP method. Sleep need starts at eight hours and adapts to your own nights; debt carries part of each shortfall forward.

Health watch

After 14 nights, Pulse flags when resting heart rate, heart rate variability and skin temperature move together in the direction associated with illness. It is an early indication, not a diagnosis.

Your data

The strap records once a second on its own. Pulse collects those readings over Bluetooth while it is open in Bluefy, stores them only in this browser on this iPhone and keeps the strap's clock correct. Export a backup now and then: deleting Bluefy or its website data deletes them.
