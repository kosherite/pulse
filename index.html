How scores work

Recovery: last night's HRV, resting heart rate, sleep and skin temperature against your last 30 nights. Strain: daily heart load, 0 to 21. Sleep: time asleep vs need, efficiency, deep and REM, timing; awake time is not counted. HR recovery: how far your heart rate drops in the minute after a workout. Estimates only, not medical advice.
