How scores are calculated

Recovery (0–100%) compares last night's heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and skin temperature with your previous 30 nights. Strain (0–21) weights each minute of the day by how hard your heart worked. Sleep (0–100%) combines time asleep against your need, efficiency, deep and REM sleep, and regular timing. Sleep stages follow the open-source NOOP method. These are independent estimates, not WHOOP's figures and not medical advice.
